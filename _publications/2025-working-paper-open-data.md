**Abstract:**  
This working paper conducts an empirical study based on open public data, exploring the application of quantitative analysis methods in real economic scenarios. The research follows the standard empirical pipeline: data cleaning, variable construction, model specification, robustness test and heterogeneity analysis, providing a reference for similar open-data research projects.
