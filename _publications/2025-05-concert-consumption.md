**Abstract:**  
This paper constructs a multi-method research framework combining web crawler text mining, questionnaire survey and structural equation modeling to systematically identify key experience pain points of Z-generation offline concert consumers. Using Python crawler, we collect high-interaction posts from Xiaohongshu and identify six core pain point dimensions via LDA topic model. Based on SOR theory, we build a behavioral mechanism model with fan attachment as the moderator variable. Empirical analysis based on valid samples shows that logistics service issues and venue hardware defects are more likely to cause immediate experiential disappointment, while ticketing fairness and process transparency tend to enhance perceived risk. The study provides decision support for precision operation and experience optimization in the live performance industry.
