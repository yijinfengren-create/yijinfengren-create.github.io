**Abstract:**  
This paper proposes a bank product subscription prediction framework oriented to telemarketing customer segmentation. We adopt the WOE-IV method for feature screening and dimensionality reduction, effectively identifying key predictive variables while handling categorical features. We compare the performance of multiple ensemble learning algorithms including random forest, gradient boosting and stacking models on real-world bank marketing datasets. The model effectively identifies high-value customer groups and provides an empirical basis for precision marketing and customer relationship management in commercial banks.
