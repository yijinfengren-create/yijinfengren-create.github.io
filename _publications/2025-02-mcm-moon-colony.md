**Abstract:**  
This paper develops an integrated optimization framework for sustainable Moon Colony transportation systems. We first construct a Cost–Time Trade-off Optimization Model under ideal operating conditions and derive the Pareto frontier via single-variable discretized exhaustive search, identifying the knee-point strategy with precise cost-time balance. We further incorporate non-ideal operational factors including Coriolis-force-induced tether constraints, tidal-force-related elevator downtime, and rocket launch failures to build a Robust Cost–Time Trade-off Optimization Model. For long-term water logistics, we propose a Dynamic Water Supply Optimization Model solved via a stage-wise optimization framework, minimizing annual transportation cost under strict survival-oriented inventory constraints. We also introduce an Environmental Impact Coefficient based on radiative forcing equivalence to compare emission impacts across strategies. Results show that the optimal knee-point strategy allocates 72.35% of materials to the space elevator system, completing construction in 164.52 years at a total cost of 296.33 trillion USD.
