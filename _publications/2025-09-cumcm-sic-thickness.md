**Abstract:**  
This paper establishes a progressive mathematical model for measuring the thickness of SiC epitaxial layers using infrared interferometry. For the single-reflection scenario, we derive a double-beam interference thickness model based on optical interference principles and the Drude model, incorporating Snell's law and Cauchy dispersion formula. We then design a systematic algorithm pipeline: spectral preprocessing, Fresnel formula inversion for refractive index, nonlinear weighted least squares fitting of Cauchy dispersion coefficients, and peak-prominence-based weighted average thickness calculation. We further derive the necessary conditions for multi-beam interference and propose a second-order derivative critical-finding algorithm. The model achieves high numerical stability with mass conservation residuals on the order of \(10^{-14}\), and provides a reliable tool for in-situ monitoring of epitaxial growth.
